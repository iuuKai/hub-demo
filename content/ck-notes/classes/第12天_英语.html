三、语法：状语从句 ⭐⭐⭐⭐

1. 什么是状语从句？

在复合句中作状语的从句，修饰主句的动词、形容词或整个句子。

结构：主句 + 连词 + 状语从句，或 连词 + 状语从句 + 主句

2. 状语从句的分类（9种）

类型常用连词意思
时间状语从句when, while, as, before, after, since, until, as soon as当…时候，在…之前/之后，自从，直到，一…就
地点状语从句where, wherever在…地方，无论哪里
原因状语从句because, since, as, now that因为，既然
目的状语从句so that, in order that为了，以便
结果状语从句so...that..., such...that...如此…以至于…
条件状语从句if, unless, as long as如果，除非，只要
让步状语从句although, though, even if, even though虽然，尽管，即使
比较状语从句than, as...as...比，和…一样
方式状语从句as, as if, as though按照，好像

3. 重点状语从句详解

（1）时间状语从句

when vs while vs as

  • when：当…时候（可指时间点或时间段）
    • When I came in, he was reading.
  • while：当…时候（只指时间段，从句用进行时）
    • While I was reading, he came in.
  • as：当…时候，一边…一边…
    • As I was walking, I saw a cat.

before / after / since / until

  • before：在…之前
    • Finish your homework before you go to bed.
  • after：在…之后
    • After he finished his work, he went home.
  • since：自从（主句用完成时）
    • I have lived here since I was born.
  • until / till：直到
    • I will wait until you come back.

as soon as：一…就…

  • As soon as I get home, I will call you.

注意：时间状语从句中，用一般现在时表示将来（主将从现）

I will tell him when he comes.（不是 will come）

（2）条件状语从句

if：如果

  • If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

unless：除非（= if...not）

  • You will fail unless you study hard.
  • = You will fail if you don't study hard.

as long as：只要

  • You can borrow the book as long as you return it.

注意：条件状语从句中，也用一般现在时表示将来（主将从现）

If it rains, we will cancel the picnic.（不是 will rain）

（3）原因状语从句

because：因为（语气最强，回答why）

  • I stayed at home because it was raining.

since / as：既然，由于（语气较弱，已知原因）

  • Since you are tired, you should rest.
  • As it was late, we went home.

now that：既然

  • Now that you have finished, you can leave.

注意：because和so不能同时用在一个句子里

❌ Because he was ill, so he didn't come.

✅ Because he was ill, he didn't come.

✅ He was ill, so he didn't come.

（4）让步状语从句

although / though：虽然，尽管

  • Although he is young, he is very smart.
  • Though it was cold, he went out without a coat.

even if / even though：即使

  • Even if it rains, we will go.
  • Even though he was tired, he kept working.

注意：although/though和but不能同时用在一个句子里

❌ Although he is poor, but he is happy.

✅ Although he is poor, he is happy.

✅ He is poor, but he is happy.

（5）目的状语从句

so that：为了，以便（从句中常用can/could/may/might）

  • He gets up early so that he can catch the bus.
  • She studied hard so that she could pass the exam.

in order that：为了（更正式）

  • He saved money in order that he could buy a car.

（6）结果状语从句

so...that...：如此…以至于…

  • so + 形容词/副词 + that
    • He is so tall that he can reach the ceiling.
    • She ran so fast that I couldn't catch her.

such...that...：如此…以至于…

  • such + 名词 + that
    • It is such a good book that I read it twice.
    • They are such nice people that everyone likes them.

（7）比较状语从句

than：比

  • She is taller than me.
  • He runs faster than his brother.

as...as...：和…一样

  • She is as tall as me.
  • He runs as fast as his brother.

not as/so...as...：不如…

  • She is not as tall as me.
  • He doesn't run as fast as his brother.

4. 主将从现规则

在时间状语从句和条件状语从句中，如果主句是一般将来时，从句用一般现在时表示将来。

主句（将来时）从句（现在时表将来）
I will call youwhen I arrive.
We will go campingif it is sunny tomorrow.
She will be happywhen she hears the news.
They won't gounless it stops raining.

四、阅读理解（真题难度）

How to Deal with Stress

Stress is a common problem in modern life. Almost everyone experiences stress at some point. While a little stress can be helpful, too much stress can cause serious health problems such as high blood pressure, heart disease, and depression.

There are many causes of stress. Work pressure is one of the biggest causes. Many people have to work long hours and meet tight deadlines. Financial problems, family issues, and major life changes can also cause stress.

Fortunately, there are many ways to reduce stress. One effective method is regular exercise. Physical activity helps your body produce endorphins, which are natural chemicals that make you feel good. Even a 30-minute walk every day can make a big difference.

Another way to manage stress is through relaxation techniques. Deep breathing, meditation, and yoga can help calm your mind and reduce anxiety. You can also try listening to soft music or taking a warm bath.

It is also important to maintain a healthy lifestyle. Getting enough sleep, eating a balanced diet, and avoiding too much caffeine and alcohol can help your body handle stress better.

Finally, don't be afraid to ask for help. Talking to a friend, family member, or professional counselor can help you feel better and find solutions to your problems.

Remember, stress is a normal part of life, but it doesn't have to control your life. With the right methods, you can manage stress effectively and live a healthier, happier life.

重点词汇：stress（压力），pressure（压力），financial（财务的），effective（有效的），endorphins（内啡肽），relaxation（放松），meditation（冥想），anxiety（焦虑），balanced（均衡的），caffeine（咖啡因），counselor（顾问）
